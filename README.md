# Wave
An aesthetic horizontal pencil holder with a lever to lift the pencil for easy access.

[Demo Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NQgvl-1CRQo)

[Onshape Link](https://cad.onshape.com/documents/e613a18cc5a871a14fb9318e/w/8ef8eb64c15deb9ffe2f0e5e/e/b199f0ddba6e296bd71b392a)


The core reason for conjuring this design was to hold my overly-expensive mechanical pencils in a way so that they are easy to reach, while also looking pleasing on a desk. This core design fundamental was achieved by incorporating various modern flairs, like wavy silhouettes and minimalistic details. Along with the external designs, however, it has a lever to make the pencil rise for and easier time grabbing it. I love how this model feels so solid as a product, and not flimsy like some 3D printed products. The model in this repo is currently V3, as it is the first successful iteration of the design, and I truly believe that this is an object that can elevate the experience of anyone who has a pen or pencil they want to show off!

The assembly is pretty straightforward, and can be done by only looking at the photos below. However, there are a few details to be wary of.
  1. There are two "fins" just below the lever on the Bambu Lab Slicer image above, and those have a slight curve at the edges so that it fits in the second spot of the bottom case in both directions.
  2. The single "fin" with the two rectangular cutouts should be fit in to where the lever will be by applying some amount of force.
  3. The orientation of the group of 21 "fins" in the lower right hand corner does not matter.
